Overview

At FreeCourseHub, accessible from our official website, user privacy is one of our primary commitments. This document outlines the categories of data recorded and how it helps optimize educational discovery.

1. Log Files & Analytics

FreeCourseHub follows standard procedures for utilizing log files. These files anonymously log visitors when they navigate the platform. Information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages to maintain site performance.

2. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like standard web services, FreeCourseHub utilizes lightweight cookies. These cookies store non-personal visitor preferences and accessed pages to enhance usability and streamline user navigation based on browser settings.

3. LocalStorage Usage

Interactive platform features, such as the My Favorites ❤️ utility, store data strictly within your browser's local storage ecosystem (LocalStorage). Your saved courses never transfer to external servers or third parties.

4. Third-Party Vendors & Advertising

Google and third-party partners may serve informative ads to visitors based on visits across the internet via cookies (such as DART cookies). Third-party advertising networks measure advertisement effectiveness independently without FreeCourseHub storing personal profile details.

5. External Educational Links

Our platform includes outbound links directing to publicly hosted courses, playlists, and materials. We encourage learners to review external third-party privacy guidelines directly upon visiting external domains.

6. Consent

By browsing and learning on FreeCourseHub, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy terms and operational standards.
